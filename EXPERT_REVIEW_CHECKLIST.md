# Expert Review Checklist

This checklist must be completed before public or clinical use. The current prototype has not been reviewed by licensed experts.

## Legal and IP Review

- [ ] Patent attorney verifies Sections 3(c), 3(d), 3(j), and 3(p) explanations.
- [ ] IP professional verifies TKDL, prior-art, WIPO, and ABS statements.
- [ ] Reviewer confirms every citation, page number, authority, and official source link.
- [ ] Reviewer approves the distinction between Indian law and international guidance.

## Medical and Botanical Review

- [ ] Qualified Ayurvedic practitioner verifies traditional-use descriptions.
- [ ] Clinician or pharmacologist verifies contraindications, interactions, and safety wording.
- [ ] Botanist verifies common names, scientific names, aliases, and image identity.
- [ ] Reviewer checks rare, protected, and potentially toxic botanicals for sourcing warnings.

## Release Gate

- [ ] Reviewers and review dates are recorded.
- [ ] The disclaimer remains visible in the dashboard and exported reports.
- [ ] No herb entry is presented as a diagnosis, prescription, or guaranteed treatment.
